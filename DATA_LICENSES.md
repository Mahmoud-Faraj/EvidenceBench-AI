# Data licenses and attribution

The repository does not redistribute the SciFact dataset. The preparation script downloads it from the
official Allen Institute for AI release, verifies its SHA-256 checksum, and writes generated files under
ignored local directories.

- SciFact claims and evidence annotations: CC BY 4.0
- SciFact corpus abstracts derived from S2ORC: ODC-By 1.0
- SciFact code: Apache License 2.0

Dataset citation:

> David Wadden, Shanchuan Lin, Kyle Lo, Lucy Lu Wang, Madeleine van Zuylen, Arman Cohan, and Hannaneh
> Hajishirzi. “Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims.” EMNLP 2020.

The exact license terms remain authoritative at:
